Play the Wind

Play the Wind: a row of hanging chimes tuned to a pentatonic scale. Press and drag through them, or the gaps beside them, to raise wind and hear them ring on their own. You can also tap, click, or press a chime's letter key to ring one directly. The Wind button or the spacebar throws a single gust across the whole field, and arrow keys raise wind from either side. Use the sound palette menu to change its mood.
