Play the Wind

Play the Wind: a row of hanging chimes tuned to an ascending major scale. Press and drag through them, or the gaps beside them, to raise wind and hear them ring on their own. You can also tap, click, or press a chime's letter key to ring one directly. The Wind button or the spacebar throws a single gust across the whole field, and arrow keys raise wind from either side. Press Q, W, or E, or click a material button, to change the chimes' material. Use the sound palette menu to change its mood.
